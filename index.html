Crossfade Mixer – Privacy Policy

This extension does not collect, store or sell personal data.

  • Settings (crossfade length, equalizer, language) are kept only on your device, in the browser's local storage.
  • The extension processes the audio on music.youtube.com locally on your device. Audio is never recorded or sent anywhere.
  • To show synced lyrics, the title, artist name and duration of the playing song are sent to the lrclib.net service. No other information is sent.
  • No ads, tracking or analytics are used. No remote code is loaded.
